How Magpigil works

  1. Park itFeel the urge to buy something? Add it first. Enter the name, price, category, and rate how badly you want it from 1 to 10. Then choose a wait of 7, 14, 30, or 60 days.
  2. WaitA ring counts down the days. You can skip an item early with “I don’t need it,” or tap “Buy anyway,” which is logged as an early purchase.
  3. DecideWhen the wait ends, the item lights up. Rate your desire again, then skip it and keep the money, or buy it because you still want it.
  4. See your resultsThe big number at the top is the money you kept. The Report tab shows each month, each category, and whether your urge usually fades after waiting.

Good to know

Private. There is no account. Your list is saved in this browser on this device only.

No reminders. The countdown updates when you open the app. Set a phone calendar reminder if you want a nudge when a wait ends.

Back it up. Use “Copy backup” on the Report tab before clearing your browser data or changing phones.

Work hours. Enter your pay per hour on the Wait list tab to see what each item costs in hours of your work.

Backup

Your list lives in this browser only. Copy a backup and keep it in your notes, or paste it here to move to a new phone.
